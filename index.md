This website displays on-going project work within the <a href="//cmmid.lshtm.ac.uk" target="_blank">Centre for the Mathematical Modelling of Infectious Diseases</a> at the <a href="//www.lshtm.ac.uk" target="_blank">London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine</a>. 

We aim to show **collaborative work in progress** on topics of broader public interest. 
We recommend interpreting results with caution, and note that work may become outdated: please note the recency of last update on each page.

We welcome feedback. 
Please contact individual authors listed on the content pages, or leave a comment on the general purpose [forum](https://github.com/cmmid/cmmid.github.io/discussions).
